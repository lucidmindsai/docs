**gAIa** is an AI companion for trees — a living map grounded in real-world data, tree growth simulations, and impact estimates. Every tree has a voice.

Built for city planners, arborists, schools, agroforesters, and tree enthusiasts.

## What gAIa does

gAIa brings together data, local knowledge, and simulations so you can explore how trees might grow and what benefits they may provide over time. Ask plain-language questions about trees and forests in your city and get data-driven answers.

## Behind the scenes

Every answer is powered by real tree data, scientific models, and open city databases.

1. **Transparent by design** — answers are grounded in real records, with data gaps flagged rather than guessed.
2. **Backed by advanced simulations** — forecasts of carbon, biodiversity, cooling, and water come from GUS, a validated agent-based simulation engine.
3. **Driven by knowledge graphs** — context comes from linked ecological data and scientific research.

## Who it's for

- **Arborists and tree managers** — prioritise maintenance, track health, and run simulations
- **City planners and green space teams** — understand ecosystem services and decide with data
- **Researchers and developers** — access structured tree data and scenarios via the API
- **Community groups and citizens** — contribute observations and explore local trees
